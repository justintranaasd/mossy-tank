# Workflow 6 — Content Repurposing

**When to use:** After an article is published and indexed. Repurpose top-performing content
into Pinterest pins, a YouTube/Shorts script, and a newsletter blurb. One article → multiple
traffic channels, all faceless.

**Fill in:** `[ARTICLE TITLE]`, `[TARGET KEYWORD]`, and paste the article content.

---

## Prompt — Pinterest (run first, highest ROI for this niche)

```
You are a Pinterest content strategist for Mossy Tank (mossytank.com), an aquascaping blog.
Pinterest audience: predominantly female (60%), 25–40, interested in home aesthetics,
plants, and pets. Aquascaping pins perform well with "before/after", "how to", and
"beginner mistake" angles.

ARTICLE TITLE: [ARTICLE TITLE]
TARGET KEYWORD: [TARGET KEYWORD]
ARTICLE CONTENT:
[PASTE ARTICLE HERE]

Create 5 Pinterest pin concepts. For each pin:

1. PIN TITLE (max 100 chars — keyword near the front, curiosity or benefit hook)
2. PIN DESCRIPTION (150–200 chars — expands on title, includes 3–5 hashtags at the end)
3. IMAGE CONCEPT (describe what the pin image should show — text overlay, background image,
   style e.g. "clean white background with photo of java fern in tank, text overlay:
   '5 Plants That NEVER Need CO2'")
4. BOARD suggestion (e.g. "Planted Aquarium Ideas", "Betta Tank Setup", "Easy Aquarium Plants")

Vary the angles across the 5 pins: how-to, listicle, mistake-to-avoid, beginner tip, aesthetic.
```

## Prompt — YouTube / Shorts script

```
You are a YouTube scriptwriter for Mossy Tank, a faceless aquascaping channel. Videos use
b-roll footage of tanks, plants, and fish — no on-camera presenter. Voiceover only.

FORMAT: [Short (60s Shorts) | Standard (8–12 min YouTube)]
ARTICLE TITLE: [ARTICLE TITLE]
ARTICLE CONTENT:
[PASTE ARTICLE HERE]

Write the script with:
- HOOK (first 3 seconds — must stop the scroll): one provocative question or surprising fact
- INTRO (10–15 seconds): promise of what they'll learn + why it matters
- MAIN CONTENT: adapt article's key sections into tight, spoken-word paragraphs
  - Add [B-ROLL: description] cues throughout (e.g. "[B-ROLL: close-up of java fern leaves]")
  - Keep sentences short — this is spoken, not read
- CTA (final 10 seconds): "Link in description for the full guide at Mossy Tank"
- TITLE OPTIONS: 3 YouTube title variants (curiosity + keyword + benefit)
- THUMBNAIL CONCEPT: describe the ideal thumbnail (text + image)

For Shorts: compress to 3 punchy tips, hook → tip 1 → tip 2 → tip 3 → CTA, max 150 words total.
```

---

## Prompt — Newsletter blurb

```
Write a newsletter blurb for the Mossy Tank email list announcing this article.

ARTICLE TITLE: [ARTICLE TITLE]
ARTICLE URL: [FULL URL]
ARTICLE CONTENT:
[PASTE ARTICLE CONTENT OR SUMMARY]

Format:
- Subject line (max 50 chars — curiosity-driven, avoid spam words)
- Preview text (max 90 chars — extends the subject line hook)
- Body (100–150 words): 
  - Open with a relatable hobbyist scenario or problem
  - 2–3 sentences on what the article covers and why it's useful
  - One specific tip or surprising fact from the article (gives value before the click)
  - CTA button text + URL
- Tone: conversational, like a note from a fellow hobbyist — not a corporate newsletter
```

---

## Repurposing priority order

1. **Pinterest** — every article, immediately on publish. Highest ROI for this audience.
2. **Newsletter** — once you have a list (Phase 3). Use for top articles only.
3. **YouTube/Shorts** — Phase 3. Start with your top 5 traffic articles repurposed as Shorts.
