# Workflow 3 — Draft Writer

**When to use:** After you have a content brief (Workflow 2). Paste the brief + this prompt
to generate a full article draft.

**Fill in:** `[PASTE FULL BRIEF HERE]` and `[PAGE TYPE]` before pasting.

**Important:** After Claude produces the draft, you MUST complete the `## Your 20%` section
below — this is the moat that makes the content rank and stay ranked.

---

## Prompt

```
You are a content writer for Mossy Tank (mossytank.com), a beginner-focused aquascaping and
planted aquarium blog. 

BRAND VOICE:
- Warm, encouraging, and honest — like advice from a more experienced hobbyist friend
- Never condescending; beginners are often anxious about killing their fish/plants
- Practical over theoretical: tell them exactly what to do, not just what's possible
- Australian/UK-friendly spelling where relevant (e.g. "colour", "behaviour") but keep it
  globally readable
- No fluff intros ("In this article we will...") — start with the hook immediately
- Short paragraphs (2–4 lines max). Use bullet lists for steps and options.
- Add genuine encouragement at key "this is scary" moments

PAGE TYPE: [pillar | supporting-article | money-page | troubleshooting]

CONTENT BRIEF:
[PASTE FULL BRIEF HERE]

Write the full draft following the brief's outline exactly. For each section:
- Write complete, publish-ready prose (not bullet notes)
- Add a [PHOTO: description of ideal photo/image here] placeholder wherever an image would help
- Add a [EXPERIENCE: specific question for the operator to answer from personal experience]
  placeholder at every E-E-A-T hook point from the brief — do NOT fill these in yourself
- Add [AFFILIATE: product name — Amazon search term] placeholders where products should be linked
- Add [INTERNAL LINK: suggested page] placeholders for internal links

For money pages (buying guides / roundups):
- Include a comparison table near the top
- Use "best for beginners", "best budget", "best overall" style categorisation
- Add an affiliate disclosure reminder at the top: [AFFILIATE DISCLOSURE COMPONENT]

End the draft with:
## Editor checklist (delete before publishing)
- [ ] All [EXPERIENCE] placeholders filled with real first-hand notes
- [ ] All [AFFILIATE] links replaced with real tracked Amazon/affiliate URLs  
- [ ] All [INTERNAL LINK] placeholders replaced with real URLs
- [ ] All [PHOTO] placeholders replaced with real images or noted for later
- [ ] Read aloud — does it sound human?
- [ ] Run Workflow 4 (On-Page SEO QA) before publishing
```

---

## Your 20% (do this before publishing — non-negotiable)

The draft Claude produces is the skeleton. Your job:

1. **Fill every `[EXPERIENCE]` placeholder** — write 2–5 sentences from your own experience.
   Even small details ("when I first tried this, the water went green within a week") build trust.
2. **Add or source one original photo per major section** — even a phone photo of your tank
   beats stock. Label it with descriptive alt text.
3. **Read the whole thing aloud** — fix anything that sounds like a robot wrote it.
4. **Fact-check any specific numbers** (temperatures, pH ranges, product specs).

This is what separates a $0 AI article from one that ranks and builds a real audience.
